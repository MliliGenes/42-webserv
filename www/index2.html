MUSIC — also known as I AM MUSIC — is the third studio album from Atlanta rapper Playboi Carti. Released March 14, 2025 through AWGE and Interscope, it marks a dramatic shift from the baby-voice approach of Whole Lotta Red toward a deeper, raspier delivery rooted in 2000s Atlanta mixtape culture.

Production credits span Ojivolta, Cardo, F1lthy, Metro Boomin, Kanye West and 808 Mafia members TM88 and Southside. Billboards reading "I AM MUSIC MF" appeared in Times Square, Downtown LA and Miami before the drop. Recording continued until hours before release. The album debuted at #1 on the Billboard 200 — Carti's fastest-selling project to date and the highest-selling rap album debut of 2025.
